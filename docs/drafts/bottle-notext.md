# Bottle Note Text Draft

These bottle notes are messages from a mysterious earlier visitor who explored the island before the player. They should guide the player at important points, but they should not explain controls or give step-by-step instructions.

The bottle count should stay limited. If every scene has a bottle, the notes stop feeling special and start feeling like a tutorial UI. Use them for important transitions, new goals, strange events, ability gates, and late-game atmosphere.

Recommended placement logic:

- Early bottles should be in believable wet places: beach sand, puddles, rain hollows, troughs, bilge water, leaky cabins.
- Later bottles can appear in stranger places: dry fountains, stone bowls, sealed niches. This should make the player suspect the visitor knew too much, or that the island moved the bottles.
- Optional bottles should be behind their matching ability gates, so the player does not receive a hint before it matters.

## Main Bottle List

1. `Wake-up Shore` / `Drift-In Sand`
   Placement: half-buried in wet sand near the first safe path.
   Purpose: introduce the visitor and explain why bottle notes exist.
   Direct meaning: Someone was here before the player, left notes to help future castaways, and may know how to escape.

   EN:
   > To the next poor soul washed onto this shore: I was here before you, with sand in my boots and fear in my teeth. I sealed what I learned in bottles, because bottles last longer than campfires and honest promises. Read on, if you mean to leave this island with all your bones. - A Former Guest

   RU:
   > Тому бедолаге, кого море выбросило на этот берег: я был здесь до тебя, с песком в сапогах и страхом в зубах. Всё, что разузнал, я запер в бутылках: бутылки живут дольше костров и честных обещаний. Читай, коли хочешь уйти с острова при всех костях. - Прежний Гость

2. `Old Door Path` / `Shut-Stone Path`
   Placement: in a puddle near the first stone door and wheel/switch route.
   Purpose: teach that stone doors respond to island mechanisms.
   Direct meaning: Closed stone doors are opened by nearby wheels or similar mechanisms.

   EN:
   > The old stone doors here are stubborn, but not deaf to work. When a door refuses to move, look for a wheel with salt in its cracks. Turn the island's old bones, and the path may open.

   RU:
   > Старые каменные двери здесь упрямы, но не глухи к делу. Если дверь не двигается, ищи штурвал с солью в трещинах. Повернёшь старые кости острова - глядишь, и тропа откроется.

3. `Jungle Exit` / `Almost-Camp Trail`
   Placement: in a rain-fed hollow near the route into Warmrocks.
   Purpose: point the player toward Warmrocks and Rikko.
   Direct meaning: Continue to the camp ahead; there is an important merchant/NPC there.

   EN:
   > Smoke ahead means someone is still warm enough to lie, trade, or help. I met a scaly merchant near those rocks. Listen to him, but count your fingers after the handshake.

   RU:
   > Дым впереди значит, что там кто-то ещё достаточно живой, чтобы врать, торговать или помочь. У тех камней я встретил чешуйчатого торговца. Выслушай его, но после рукопожатия пересчитай пальцы.

4. `Campfire and Shop` / `The Warm Deal`
   Placement: tucked into a cracked water jar near the campfire/shop area.
   Purpose: frame the hub as a place to rest, recover, and prepare.
   Direct meaning: Campfires and the shop are preparation points before dangerous areas.

   EN:
   > A fire on this island is more than warmth. It is a place to breathe, mend, and stop pretending an empty pocket is bravery. Buy what keeps you standing before the next dark mouth swallows you.

   RU:
   > Костёр на этом острове - больше чем тепло. Здесь можно выдохнуть, подлататься и перестать делать вид, будто пустой карман - это храбрость. Купи то, что удержит тебя на ногах, прежде чем следующая тёмная пасть тебя проглотит.

5. `Ship Approach` / `Dead Captain's Landing`
   Placement: washed against the wreck landing, beside broken planks and tide pools.
   Purpose: confirm the ship as the next destination and hint that the player needs a weapon.
   Direct meaning: The ship is the correct next area, and the player should find the sabre there.

   EN:
   > That wreck is the wrong ship, but the right trouble. I found a blade there, and soon after I learned why the ship had kept it close. Do not wander its planks empty-handed for long.

   RU:
   > Этот корабль не тот, зато беда на нём самая нужная. Там я нашёл клинок, а вскоре понял, почему корабль держал его рядом. Не броди по его доскам с пустыми руками слишком долго.

6. `Deck Skirmish` / `Bitefoot Deck`
   Placement: in a rain barrel or broken deck drain after the sabre pickup.
   Purpose: warn that the ship has living threats without giving combat tactics.
   Direct meaning: The ship deck is dangerous; expect creatures ahead.

   EN:
   > First I heard wet feet on dry planks. Then came the chewing. If the deck starts smacking and slurping in the dark, keep your steel close and your fingers closer.

   RU:
   > Сперва я услышал мокрые лапы на сухих досках. Потом началось чавканье. Если палуба в темноте зашлёпала и зачмокала, держи сталь близко, а пальцы ещё ближе.

7. `Bilge Collapse` / `Bad Floor`
   Placement: just before the weak floor, in dirty bilge runoff.
   Purpose: warn that the ship may trap the player soon.
   Direct meaning: A one-way fall or trap is coming; the player should be ready before continuing.

   EN:
   > Some decks creak from age. Some creak like they are hungry. If the floor below you starts singing too sweetly, take a breath and check your supplies. The hold below does not give easy walks back.

   RU:
   > Одни палубы скрипят от старости. Другие скрипят так, будто проголодались. Если пол под ногами запел слишком ласково, вдохни и проверь припасы. Трюм внизу не любит лёгких дорог назад.

8. `Cargo Mechanism Loop` / `Turnbelly Hold`
   Placement: trapped in a bilge grate near the central cargo mechanism.
   Purpose: hint that both ship wheels are needed.
   Direct meaning: Find and activate two ship wheels to open the route forward.

   EN:
   > The captain loved wheels. One for the helm, two for his locks, and none for his conscience. If one wheel leaves the way shut, seek its twin deeper in the hold.

   RU:
   > Капитан любил штурвалы. Один - для руля, два - для замков, и ни одного - для совести. Если один штурвал оставил путь закрытым, ищи его близнеца глубже в трюме.

9. `Captain's Cabin and Arena` / `Captain's Last Laugh`
   Placement: near the pre-boss campfire or supply chest, in a cracked decanter.
   Purpose: prepare the player for a major encounter and hint at the hook reward.
   Direct meaning: A major fight is ahead. The captain spirit guards the grappling hook.

   EN:
   > The dead captain keeps what he stole, and one stolen thing may yet carry you upward. I saw iron claws among his treasures. Rest before you knock. The dead hate visitors who still breathe.

   RU:
   > Мёртвый капитан держит при себе всё, что украл, а одна украденная вещь ещё может унести тебя наверх. Среди его добра я видел железные когти. Отдохни, прежде чем стучать. Мёртвые не любят гостей, которые всё ещё дышат.

10. `Hook Escape and Teleporter` / `Sighing Shaft`
    Placement: caught high in a damp shaft, reachable only after the grappling hook.
    Purpose: validate the hook and foreshadow high routes elsewhere.
    Direct meaning: The grappling hook opens high routes and vertical paths. Look for anchors above.

    EN:
    > Ah, the iron claw found another hand. The island hangs many secrets from anchors, as if the sky once moored here. From now on, look up. Some doors pretend to be ceilings.

    RU:
    > Ага, железный коготь нашёл новую руку. Остров вешает многие тайны на якоря, будто само небо когда-то швартовалось здесь. С этого часа смотри вверх: некоторые двери притворяются потолком.

11. `Candle Gate` / `Little-Star Mouth`
    Placement: inside the firefly-gated hub shortcut/reward area, not before fireflies are obtained.
    Purpose: explain fireflies as remote light in a lore-friendly way.
    Direct meaning: Fireflies can reach places the player cannot and can light candles or activate distant light objects.

    EN:
    > Fire in a jar is not fire in a hand. Let the little stars wander where your boots cannot. Candles here are lonely things; they wake for small bright company.

    RU:
    > Огонь в банке - не то же, что огонь в руке. Пусть малые звёзды летят туда, куда сапоги не пролезут. Здешние свечи одиноки: они просыпаются от маленькой яркой компании.

12. `Split Mechanism Court` / `Two-Stone Court`
    Placement: in a dry fountain bowl, suspiciously far from open water.
    Purpose: guide the player through the two-branch ruins objective.
    Direct meaning: Cloud Mountain has two mechanism branches; both must be completed to affect the sanctuary entrance.

    EN:
    > Strange place for a bottle, aye? Stranger place for a dry fountain. This court has two old duties left unfinished. Finish both, and something under Warmrocks may finally move.

    RU:
    > Странное место для бутылки, верно? Ещё страннее - сухой фонтан. У этого двора остались два старых дела. Закончишь оба - и что-то под Тёплыми Камнями наконец сдвинется.

13. `Dark Hook Cave` / `Blindglow Cave`
    Placement: just inside the dark cave, near dripping rock or an underground pool.
    Purpose: warn about darkness and reinforce firefly use.
    Direct meaning: Use fireflies in dark caves to reveal anchors, hazards, and hidden paths.

    EN:
    > I stepped into this dark and heard wet clicking, soft wings, and stone breathing close by. No honest sailor would go farther without a light that can scout ahead.

    RU:
    > Я шагнул в эту темноту и услышал мокрое щёлканье, тихие крылья и каменное дыхание совсем рядом. Честный моряк не полезет дальше без огня, который умеет разведать путь впереди.

14. `Candle Mechanism Exit` / `Glowstone Door`
    Placement: near the exit teleporter or final candle mechanism, in a stone basin.
    Purpose: confirm the sanctuary is open and point the player back to Warmrocks.
    Direct meaning: Return to Warmrocks/Sanctuary Approach. Upgrade and buy supplies before entering the sanctuary.

    EN:
    > Hear the stone under the camp shifting? The old door has tasted enough starlight to loosen its jaw. Return to the skull-watch stones, but do not arrive poor, hungry, or too proud to mend your coat.

    RU:
    > Слышишь, как камень под лагерем шевелится? Старая дверь наглоталась звёздного света и разжала челюсть. Возвращайся к черепьим камням, но не приходи бедным, голодным и слишком гордым, чтобы залатать куртку.

15. `Final Campfire and Golem Arena` / `Last Warm Floor`
    Placement: near the final campfire, in a ceremonial bowl with a little water.
    Purpose: prepare the player for the final guardian.
    Direct meaning: The final boss is ahead. The guardian is not evil; it protects the Golden Skull.

    EN:
    > The guardian beyond is not wicked. That is the worst of it. A wicked thing can be fooled. A faithful stone only does its duty. If you still want the Skull, make ready.

    RU:
    > Страж впереди не злой. В том-то и беда. Злую тварь можно обмануть. Верный камень просто служит. Если Череп всё ещё тебе нужен, готовься.

16. `Golden Skull Exit` / `Deep Hush`
    Placement: beside the sanctuary teleporter or treasure chamber exit, impossibly dry and clean.
    Purpose: direct the player back to Rikko and add final mystery around the visitor.
    Direct meaning: The player has the Golden Skull and should return to Rikko to finish the game. The visitor hints that escape may not have fully freed them.

    EN:
    > So the Skull travels again. Carry it back to the smiling lizard if escape is still what you want. As for me, I learned that leaving an island and being free of it are not always the same voyage.

    RU:
    > Значит, Череп снова отправился в путь. Неси его к улыбающемуся ящеру, если побег всё ещё тебе нужен. А я узнал: покинуть остров и освободиться от него - не всегда одно и то же плавание.

## Optional Backtracking Bottles

These notes should only be placed behind their matching ability gates, so the player finds the hint when it is useful.

1. `Vine-Covered Alcove` / `Greenknife Nook`
   Required ability: sabre.
   Placement: behind or just past the vine blocker.
   Purpose: reward the player for returning with the sabre.
   Direct meaning: Sabre can clear greenknife vines and open this optional reward path.

   EN:
   > Greenknife vines hate steel. Once you carry a blade, the jungle remembers it can step aside.

   RU:
   > Лозы-зеленоножи ненавидят сталь. Когда у тебя есть клинок, джунгли вспоминают, что могут посторониться.

2. `Secret Overlook` / `Moon-Eye Overlook`
   Required ability: grappling hook, with extra value from fireflies.
   Placement: on the high ledge, not visible from the main intro path.
   Purpose: reward hook/firefly backtracking in the intro area.
   Direct meaning: Hook reaches the high ledge; fireflies may reveal or solve an extra secret nearby.

   EN:
   > I left this one where only a sky-minded fool would look. Anchors lift you to the Moon-Eye. The cave below keeps a darker secret for anyone carrying stars in a jar.

   RU:
   > Эту я оставил там, куда заглянет только дурень с мыслями о небе. Якоря поднимут к Лунному Глазу. Пещера ниже держит тайну потемнее для тех, кто носит звёзды в банке.

3. `Upper Cargo Hold` / `High Cargo Nest`
   Required ability: grappling hook.
   Placement: beside the optional chest or parrot cage.
   Purpose: reward returning to the ship with the hook.
   Direct meaning: Return to the ship with the hook to reach an upper optional reward, possibly including the parrot.

   EN:
   > The ship has a second belly above the first. Captains love hiding things where honest boots cannot reach. If something squawks up here, be kind. A sharp beak remembers favors.

   RU:
   > У корабля есть второе брюхо над первым. Капитаны любят прятать добро там, куда честный сапог не достанет. Если здесь что-то каркнет, будь добр. Острый клюв помнит услуги.

4. `Hard Anchor Chain` / `Brave Fool's Way`
   Required ability: grappling hook.
   Placement: after the hardest optional hook sequence, near the diamond reward.
   Purpose: reward optional hook mastery.
   Direct meaning: This is an optional hook challenge with a diamond reward.

   EN:
   > Brave Fool's Way, I called this. Then I crossed it twice, which tells you more about me than the path. Take the stone that shines and tell no one how loudly you yelled.

   RU:
   > Путём Храброго Дурака я это назвал. Потом прошёл дважды, что больше говорит обо мне, чем о тропе. Забирай блестящий камень и никому не рассказывай, как громко ты орал.
