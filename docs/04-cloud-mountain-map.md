## Scene Reference

### 1. First Anchor Gap / Birdless Gap

Traversal role:

- Entry from Warmrocks.
- First safe hook traversal lesson.

POI spec:

- Single obvious gap.
- One clear anchor point.
- Safe floor before and after the gap.
- Optional coin arc showing the intended path.

Challenges and hazards:

- Grappling hook attach, swing, and release.
- No spikes under the first required swing.
- No enemies.

Items and rewards:

- Progress.
- Optional coins.

### 2. Spike Swing Bridge / Needlewind Bridge

Traversal role:

- Adds real hazard below the hook path.

POI spec:

- Hook route over spikes.
- Safe retry platform before the hazard.
- Landing platform with enough space to recover.
- Optional coins only on the intended safe arc.

Challenges and hazards:

- Spikes below traversal path.
- Hook timing under low pressure.

Items and rewards:

- Progress.

### 3. Vertical Ruin Shaft / Ancestor's Throat

Traversal role:

- Turns Cloud Mountain into a vertical location.

POI spec:

- Chained anchors upward.
- Small rest ledges between anchor groups.
- Visual ruin walls or old stone throat shape.
- Optional coin or bottle note halfway up.

Challenges and hazards:

- Repeated hook use.
- Fall recovery space.
- No enemy pressure unless testing shows the scene is too empty.

Items and rewards:

- Upper route access.
- Optional coins.

### 4. Totem Terrace / Meanstone Terrace

Traversal role:

- First hook movement under ranged pressure.

POI spec:

- Spike totem placed to cover part of the route.
- Platforms that let the player pause out of the firing lane.
- Chest or coin pocket after the threat.

Challenges and hazards:

- Spike totem.
- Hook traversal while watching ranged timing.

Items and rewards:

- Chest or coins.
- Progress to Two-Stone Court.

### 5. Split Mechanism Court / Two-Stone Court

Traversal role:

- Central objective hub inside Cloud Mountain.
- Shows that two routes must be solved.

POI spec:

- Central safe platform.
- Two sealed or inactive route markers.
- Door or stone face that reacts after both wheel branches are complete.
- Optional visible branch to Brave Fool's Way.

Challenges and hazards:

- Route reading.
- No heavy combat in the central court.

Items and rewards:

- Route logic starts.
- Later opens path to Blindglow Cave.

### 6. Twin Wheel Branches / Forked Stone Ways

Traversal role:

- Main objective branches.
- Completes the two-wheel requirement.

POI spec:

- West route with shell projectile pressure and narrow traversal.
- East route with sea star or fire skull floor pressure.
- Steering wheel 1.
- Steering wheel 2.
- Return path back to Two-Stone Court after each branch.

Challenges and hazards:

- Shell.
- Sea star or fire skull.
- Hook movement under pressure.
- Branch navigation.

Items and rewards:

- Steering wheel 1 activated.
- Steering wheel 2 activated.
- Main route opens after both are active.

### 7. Hard Anchor Chain / Brave Fool's Way

Traversal role:

- Optional mastery branch from the central court.

POI spec:

- Long high traversal chain.
- Spikes or pit below.
- Reward chest at the end.
- Return route back to the court.

Challenges and hazards:

- Long hook sequence.
- Spike danger.
- No mandatory progression.

Items and rewards:

- 1 diamond.
- Optional coins or rum if extra reward is needed.

### 8. Dark Hook Cave / Blindglow Cave

Traversal role:

- Dark traversal scene after the mechanism objective.

POI spec:

- Low global light, but readable silhouettes.
- Fireflies reveal anchors or safe route hints.
- Small candle or glow target that improves readability.
- Clear exit toward Glowstone Door.

Challenges and hazards:

- Darkness.
- Hidden or low-visibility anchors.
- Spikes or fire skulls used sparingly.

Items and rewards:

- Progress.
- Optional coins if they help guide the route.

### 9. Candle Mechanism Exit / Glowstone Door

Traversal role:

- Final Cloud Mountain mechanism.
- Opens Skullkeeper Hollow from Warmrocks.
- Returns the player to Warmrocks.

POI spec:

- Candle switch or lightcatcher.
- Stone mechanism that visibly opens the sanctuary route.
- Ruins teleporter activation.
- Exit back to Warmrocks.

Challenges and hazards:

- Firefly activation of unreachable objects.
- No heavy enemy pressure after the puzzle is solved.

Items and rewards:

- Sanctuary route opens.
- Teleporter / fast return to Warmrocks.
- Optional main-path diamond if upgrade pacing needs it.

## Layout Notes

- Cloud Mountain must read as an upward climb from Warmrocks.
- The central court sits high in the location, with branches around it.
- After `Glowstone Door`, the player returns to Warmrocks and then travels east
  to Skullkeeper Hollow.
