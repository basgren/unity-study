## Scene Reference

### 1. Sanctuary Entrance / Skullkeeper Door

Traversal role:

- Entry from Warmrocks after Cloud Mountain opens the route.

POI spec:

- Large stone entrance door.
- Visual confirmation that the earlier sanctuary lock is now open.
- First final-location combat pocket.
- Optional note or carving warning about the Skullkeeper.

Challenges and hazards:

- Light combat.
- One basic enemy or low-pressure trap.
- No new rule introduction.

Items and rewards:

- Progress.
- Optional small healing before the final gauntlet begins.

### 2. Spike and Anchor Hall / Bonewind Hall

Traversal role:

- Late-game hook precision test.

POI spec:

- Hook route over spikes.
- Smaller landing margins than earlier locations.
- Safe retry point before the hardest jump.
- Visual bone/stone wind shape to sell the room name.

Challenges and hazards:

- Hook traversal.
- Spikes.
- Late-game precision.

Items and rewards:

- Progress.

### 3. Candle Hall / Star Nest Hall

Traversal role:

- Final firefly candle sequence.

POI spec:

- Several candles in a readable sequence.
- Door that opens only after enough candles are lit.
- At least one candle placed beyond direct player reach.
- Permanent lit state after completion.

Challenges and hazards:

- Firefly targeting and timing.
- Light puzzle at final complexity.
- Minimal combat so the puzzle remains readable.

Items and rewards:

- Main door opens.
- Optional coins or healing after completion.

### 4. Projectile Gallery / Stormwalk Gallery

Traversal role:

- Final projectile pressure scene.

POI spec:

- Shells and cannons in layered lanes.
- Barrel cover or safe pockets.
- Clear movement rhythm through the gallery.
- Exit to Murmur Tower.

Challenges and hazards:

- Shell projectiles.
- Cannon fire.
- Pattern reading under pressure.

Items and rewards:

- Progress.
- Optional supply chest after the pressure lane.

### 5. Totem Tower / Murmur Tower

Traversal role:

- Last traversal pressure before optional treasure and final boss.

POI spec:

- Vertical route.
- Totem shrine or old mouth-stone pressure source.
- Spawned threats in controlled amounts.
- Branch to Don't-Tell Room before the final campfire.

Challenges and hazards:

- Big-mouth or bird totem.
- Fire skulls or explosive flies.
- Vertical traversal under pressure.

Items and rewards:

- Route to final campfire.
- Optional chest or coins if the tower needs a reward beat.

### 6. Treasure Annex / Don't-Tell Room

Traversal role:

- Optional late-game mastery branch before final boss.

POI spec:

- Secret entrance or side route from Murmur Tower.
- Dark treasure space.
- Candles or glow targets.
- Reward chest placed after the hardest hazard.

Challenges and hazards:

- Fireflies required.
- Spikes.
- Bird totem or sea star.
- Optional high-pressure combat/traversal mix.

Items and rewards:

- 2 diamonds.
- Rare rum.
- Large healing bottle.
- Coins if economy pacing needs it.

### 7. Final Campfire and Golem Arena / Last Warm Floor

Traversal role:

- Final preparation scene and Stone Golem boss.

POI spec:

- Final campfire before the fight trigger.
- Supply chest or shop-adjacent supply point if needed.
- Clear boss trigger boundary.
- Stone Golem arena.
- Door to Deep Hush opens after victory.

Challenges and hazards:

- Stone Golem boss.
- Laser, boomerang arm, ground slam, phase 2 pressure.

Items and rewards:

- Checkpoint.
- Supplies before boss.
- Golden Skull chamber access after victory.

### 8. Golden Skull Exit / Deep Hush

Traversal role:

- Final reward and return.

POI spec:

- Quiet Golden Skull chamber.
- Golden Skull pickup.
- Sanctuary teleporter or clear return route.
- No combat after the boss.

Challenges and hazards:

- None.
- Short calm scene after the final fight.

Items and rewards:

- Golden Skull.
- Return to Warmrocks.

## Layout Notes

- Skullkeeper Hollow moves eastward from Warmrocks and should feel like a final
  push away from the hub.
- `Don't-Tell Room` branches before `Last Warm Floor` so its rewards can still
  help with the boss.
- `Deep Hush` returns the player to Warmrocks after the final objective.
